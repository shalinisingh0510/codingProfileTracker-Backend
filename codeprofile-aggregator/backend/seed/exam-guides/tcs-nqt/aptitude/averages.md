# Averages (TCS NQT Aptitude)

## 1. Core Formula
```text
Average = Sum of observations / Number of observations
Sum = Average * Count
```

---

## 2. Weighted / Combined Average (Very common)
If group 1: (n1, avg1) and group 2: (n2, avg2)
```text
Combined average = (n1*avg1 + n2*avg2) / (n1+n2)
```

**Step-by-step example**
- 10 students avg 60, 20 students avg 50  
- Combined = (10*60 + 20*50)/30 = (600+1000)/30 = 53.33

---

## 3. Quick Tricks

### Trick 1: Average change on replacement
If one value x is replaced by y in n numbers:
```text
New average = Old average + (y - x)/n
```

### Trick 2: If average increases by d
```text
Total sum increases by n*d
```

## 4. Practice Questions (10)
**Q1.** Average of 8 numbers is 15. Find their sum.  
**Q2.** Sum of 12 numbers is 300. Find average.  
**Q3.** Average of 5 numbers is 20. One number is removed and new average is 18. Find removed number.  
**Q4.** Average of 10 numbers is 25. If average increases by 2, find new sum.  
**Q5.** Average age of 6 people is 24. If one person aged 30 leaves, find new average.  
**Q6.** Average of 9 numbers is 14. If one number is replaced by another 8 greater, find new average.  
**Q7.** A class of 20 students avg 60, another class of 30 students avg 50. Find combined average.  
**Q8.** Average of 3 numbers is 12. Two numbers are 10 and 15. Find third number.  
**Q9.** Average speed of a car for 120 km is 60 km/h. Time taken?  
**Q10.** A batsman scores 240 runs in 6 innings. What average must he score in next 4 innings to make overall average 50?

---

## 5. Answers
**A1.** Sum = 8*15 = 120  
**A2.** 300/12 = 25  
**A3.** Sum=5*20=100. New sum=4*18=72. Removed=28  
**A4.** New avg=27 => new sum=10*27=270  
**A5.** Old sum=6*24=144. New sum=114. New avg=114/5=22.8  
**A6.** Increase in sum=8. New avg=14+8/9=14.888...  
**A7.** (20*60 + 30*50)/50 = (1200+1500)/50 = 54  
**A8.** Sum=36 => third=36-25=11  
**A9.** Time = 120/60 = 2 hours  
**A10.** Target total runs = 50*10 = 500. Current runs = 240. Needed = 260 in 4 innings => required average = 65
