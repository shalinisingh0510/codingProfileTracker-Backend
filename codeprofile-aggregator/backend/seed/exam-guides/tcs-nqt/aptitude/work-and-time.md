# Work and Time (TCS NQT Aptitude)

## 1. Core Model
Work problems are easiest using **rates**.

```text
If A finishes work in x days, A's 1-day work = 1/x
If A and B work together: rate = 1/x + 1/y
Time = 1 / (rate)
```

---

## 2. LCM Shortcut (fastest for 2-3 people)
Assume total work = LCM(x, y, z).
Then compute daily work as integers.

**Step-by-step example**
A: 10 days, B: 15 days
- Total work = LCM(10,15)=30 units
- A per day = 3, B per day = 2
- Together = 5 per day => time = 30/5 = 6 days

---

## 3. Efficiency Ratio
If A is k times as efficient as B:
```text
Time ratio = 1/k
```
Example: A is 2x efficient => A takes half the time.

---

## 4. Pipes & Cisterns
Treat **inlet** as positive work and **outlet** as negative.

```text
Net rate = inlet rate - outlet rate
```

---

## 5. Practice Questions (10)
**Q1.** A can do a work in 12 days. Find A's 1-day work.  
**Q2.** A can do in 10 days and B in 15 days. Together, how many days?  
**Q3.** A can do in 18 days and B in 12 days. Together, how many days?  
**Q4.** A alone can do in 20 days. A works 5 days, then B finishes in 15 days. In how many days can B do alone?  
**Q5.** A is 3 times as efficient as B. Together they finish in 12 days. Find B's time alone.  
**Q6.** A can do in 16 days, B in 24 days. A works alone for 4 days, then A+B together. Total time?  
**Q7.** Two pipes fill a tank in 12 and 18 minutes. Together, how long?  
**Q8.** A pipe fills in 10 hours, another fills in 15 hours, and an outlet empties in 30 hours. Net fill time?  
**Q9.** A and B together do in 8 days, A alone in 12 days. Find B alone.  
**Q10.** 6 workers finish a job in 15 days. How many workers needed to finish in 10 days?

---

## 6. Answers
**A1.** 1/12  
**A2.** rate=1/10+1/15=1/6 => 6 days  
**A3.** rate=1/18+1/12=5/36 => 36/5 = 7.2 days  
**A4.** A's 5-day work = 5/20=1/4. Remaining=3/4 done by B in 15 days => B rate = (3/4)/15=1/20 => B alone 20 days  
**A5.** Let B rate = r, A rate = 3r. Together 4r = 1/12 => r=1/48 => B time = 48 days  
**A6.** A does 4/16=1/4. Remaining=3/4. A+B rate = 1/16+1/24=5/48. Time for remaining = (3/4)/(5/48)= (3/4)*(48/5)=36/5=7.2. Total = 11.2 days  
**A7.** rate=1/12+1/18=5/36 => time=36/5=7.2 minutes  
**A8.** rate=1/10+1/15-1/30 = (3+2-1)/30 = 4/30=2/15 => time=15/2=7.5 hours  
**A9.** B rate = 1/8 - 1/12 = 1/24 => B time = 24 days  
**A10.** Work=6*15=90 worker-days => workers=90/10=9
