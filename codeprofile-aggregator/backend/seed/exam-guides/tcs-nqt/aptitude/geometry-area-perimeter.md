# Geometry, Area and Perimeter (TCS NQT Prep)

## 1. Formulas Checklist

### 2D Geometric Shapes

* **Triangle**: Area = 

$$\frac{1}{2} \times b \times h$$

. Hero's formula: 

$$\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$$

 where $s = (a+b+c)/2$.

* **Rectangle**: Area = $l \times w$, Perimeter = $2(l+w)$.

* **Square**: Area = $a^2$, Perimeter = $4a$, Diagonal = $a\sqrt{2}$.

* **Circle**: Area = $\pi r^2$, Circumference = $2\pi r$.

### 3D Geometric Volumes

* **Cube**: Volume = $a^3$, Surface Area = $6a^2$.

* **Cone**: Volume = 

$$\frac{1}{3} \pi r^2 h$$

, Curved Surface Area = $\pi r l$ where $l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2}$.

* **Sphere**: Volume = 

$$\frac{4}{3} \pi r^3$$

, Surface Area = $4\pi r^2$.

## 2. Practice Questions (10)

**Q1.** The length of a rectangle is increased by 20% and its width is decreased by 10%. Find the percentage change in its area.

**Q2.** Find the area of a triangle whose sides are 13 cm, 14 cm and 15 cm.

**Q3.** The ratio of the area of a square to that of the square drawn on its diagonal is?

**Q4.** The circumference of a circle is 44 cm. Find its area.

**Q5.** If the radius of a sphere is doubled, what is the ratio of its new volume to its original volume?

**Q6.** The volume of a cylinder of height 14 cm is 176 $\text{cm}^3$. Find its radius.

**Q7.** Find the length of the longest rod that can be placed in a room 12m long, 9m broad and 8m high.

**Q8.** If the area of an equilateral triangle is $16\sqrt{3}\text{ cm}^2$, find its perimeter.

**Q9.** The diameter of a wheel is 70 cm. How many revolutions will it make to cover 660m?

**Q10.** The surface area of a cube is 150 $\text{cm}^2$. Find its volume.

## 3. Answers & Detailed Explanations

**A1.** **8% increase**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Using successive percentage change formula:
  

$$\text{Net Change} = 20 - 10 + \frac{20 \times (-10)}{100} = 10 - 2 = 8\%$$

**A2.** **84 $\text{cm}^2$**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  $s = (13+14+15)/2 = 21$ cm.
  

$$\text{Area} = \sqrt{21 \times (21-13) \times (21-14) \times (21-15)} = \sqrt{21 \times 8 \times 7 \times 6} = \sqrt{7056} = 84\text{ cm}^2$$

**A3.** **1:2**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Let side of square be $a$. Area = $a^2$.
  Diagonal of square = $a\sqrt{2}$.
  Area of square on diagonal = $(a\sqrt{2})^2 = 2a^2$.
  Ratio = $a^2 : 2a^2 = 1:2$.

**A4.** **154 $\text{cm}^2$**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  $2\pi r = 44 \implies 2 \times (22/7) \times r = 44 \implies r = 7$ cm.
  Area = $\pi r^2 = (22/7) \times 7 \times 7 = 154\text{ cm}^2$.

**A5.** **8:1**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Volume is proportional to $r^3$.
  Ratio of volumes = $(2r)^3 : r^3 = 8:1$.

**A6.** **2 cm**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Volume = $\pi r^2 h \implies 176 = (22/7) \times r^2 \times 14 \implies 176 = 44 \times r^2 \implies r^2 = 4 \implies r = 2$ cm.

**A7.** **17m**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Longest rod length = Diagonal of cuboid = $\sqrt{l^2 + w^2 + h^2}$.
  

$$\text{Length} = \sqrt{12^2 + 9^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{144 + 81 + 64} = \sqrt{289} = 17\text{m}$$

**A8.** **24 cm**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Equilateral triangle Area = $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} a^2 = 16\sqrt{3} \implies a^2 = 64 \implies a = 8$ cm.
  Perimeter = $3a = 3 \times 8 = 24$ cm.

**A9.** **300 revolutions**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Circumference = $\pi d = (22/7) \times 70 = 220$ cm = 2.2m.
  Revolutions = Total Distance / Circumference = 660m / 2.2m = 300.

**A10.** **125 $\text{cm}^3$**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Surface Area = $6a^2 = 150 \implies a^2 = 25 \implies a = 5$ cm.
  Volume = $a^3 = 5^3 = 125\text{ cm}^3$.
