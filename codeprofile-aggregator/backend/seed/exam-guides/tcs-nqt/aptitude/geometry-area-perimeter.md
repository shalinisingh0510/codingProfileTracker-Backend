# Geometry, Area and Perimeter (TCS NQT Aptitude)

## 1. Must-know Formulas

### Rectangle
```text
Area = l*w
Perimeter = 2(l+w)
```

### Square
```text
Area = a^2
Perimeter = 4a
Diagonal = a*sqrt(2)
```

### Triangle
```text
Area = (1/2)*b*h
```

### Circle
```text
Circumference = 2*pi*r
Area = pi*r^2
```

---

## 2. Quick Tips
- Convert units before solving (cm ↔ m).
- For composite shapes, break into rectangles/triangles.
- Use π = 22/7 when radius is multiple of 7; else π ≈ 3.14.

---

## 3. Practice Questions (10)
**Q1.** Find area of rectangle 12cm × 8cm.  
**Q2.** Find perimeter of square of side 9cm.  
**Q3.** Find area of triangle with base 10cm and height 6cm.  
**Q4.** Radius of circle is 7cm. Find circumference (π=22/7).  
**Q5.** Radius of circle is 14cm. Find area (π=22/7).  
**Q6.** A rectangular plot is 30m by 20m. Find its perimeter.  
**Q7.** Find diagonal of square of side 5cm.  
**Q8.** The circumference of a circle is 44cm. Find radius (π=22/7).  
**Q9.** A square and rectangle have equal perimeters. Square side is 10cm. Rectangle length is 12cm. Find rectangle breadth.  
**Q10.** Find area of a semicircle of radius 7cm (π=22/7).

---

## 4. Answers
**A1.** 96 cm²  
**A2.** 36 cm  
**A3.** 30 cm²  
**A4.** 2*(22/7)*7 = 44 cm  
**A5.** (22/7)*14^2 = 616 cm²  
**A6.** 2(30+20)=100 m  
**A7.** 5*sqrt(2) cm  
**A8.** 2*pi*r=44 => r=44/(2*(22/7))=7 cm  
**A9.** Square perimeter=40. Rectangle: 2(12+b)=40 => b=8 cm  
**A10.** (1/2)*pi*r^2 = 77 cm²
