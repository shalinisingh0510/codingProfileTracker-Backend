# Clocks, Calendar and Ages (TCS NQT Aptitude)

## 1. Ages (core patterns)
If present ages ratio is a:b and total is S:
```text
A = S * a/(a+b)
B = S * b/(a+b)
```

If difference is D and ratio is a:b:
```text
a parts - b parts = D  =>  1 part = D/(a-b)
```

---

## 2. Clocks (angles)
Angle between hour and minute hand at H:M:
```text
Angle = |30H - 5.5M| degrees
```
(Take smaller angle: min(angle, 360-angle))

---

## 3. Calendar (fast approach)
- Use odd days method (if you practice it), or use a known reference year + day shifts.
- For TCS NQT, calendar questions are usually light; focus on “day after N days” types.

---

## 4. Practice Questions (10)
**Q1.** Ages of A and B are in ratio 3:5 and sum is 64. Find ages.  
**Q2.** A is 4 years older than B. If A:B = 5:3, find ages.  
**Q3.** Average age of 5 people is 24. If a new person aged 34 joins, new average?  
**Q4.** What is the angle between hands at 3:00?  
**Q5.** What is the angle between hands at 6:30?  
**Q6.** At what time between 2 and 3 o’clock are the hands together? (in minutes after 2)  
**Q7.** If today is Monday, what day will it be after 45 days?  
**Q8.** If a person is born on a Tuesday, what day will be after 100 days?  
**Q9.** A father is 3 times as old as his son. After 12 years, he will be twice as old. Find present ages.  
**Q10.** Find smaller angle between hands at 9:20.

---

## 5. Answers
**A1.** A=24, B=40  
**A2.** Difference in parts=2 => 1 part=2 => A=10, B=6  
**A3.** Sum=120, new sum=154 => avg=154/6=25.666...  
**A4.** 90°  
**A5.** |30*6 - 5.5*30| = |180 - 165| = 15°  
**A6.** Solve 30H - 5.5M = 0 with H=2 => 60 - 5.5M = 0 => M = 120/11 = 10 10/11 minutes  
**A7.** 45 mod 7 = 3 => Thursday  
**A8.** 100 mod 7 = 2 => Thursday  
**A9.** Let son=x, father=3x. After 12: 3x+12=2(x+12) => x=12 => father=36  
**A10.** |30*9 - 5.5*20| = |270 - 110| = 160° (smaller)
