# Clocks, Calendar and Ages (TCS NQT Prep)

## 1. Clocks Formulas

* **Angle between Hands**: The angle between the hour hand and minute hand at $H$ hours and $M$ minutes is:

  

$$\text{Angle} = |30H - 5.5M| \text{ degrees}$$

  *If this angle is greater than 180°, take the reflex angle: $360^\circ - \text{Angle}$.*

* **Speed of Hands**:

  * Minute Hand Speed = $6^\circ$ per minute.
  * Hour Hand Speed = $0.5^\circ$ per minute.
  * Relative Speed of Minute hand with respect to Hour hand = $5.5^\circ$ per minute.

---

## 2. Calendar Concept (Odd Days)

* **Ordinary Year**: 365 days = 52 weeks + 1 odd day.

* **Leap Year**: 366 days = 52 weeks + 2 odd days.

* **Century Odd Days**:

  * 100 years = 5 odd days.
  * 200 years = 3 odd days.
  * 300 years = 1 odd day.
  * 400 years = 0 odd days.

---

## 3. Ages Shortcut

* Write down ratio equations directly: If present ages are in ratio $a:b$, assume ages to be $ax$ and $bx$.

---

## 4. Solved Examples

### Example 1:

Find the angle between the hands of a clock at 8:20.

* **Solution**:

  Using the angle formula with $H = 8, M = 20$:
  

$$\text{Angle} = |30 \times 8 - 5.5 \times 20| = |240 - 110| = 130^\circ$$

## 5. Practice Questions (10)

**Q1.** What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 3:40?

**Q2.** At what time between 4 and 5 o'clock will the hands of a clock be together?

**Q3.** If today is Monday, what day of the week will it be after 61 days?

**Q4.** The ratio of ages of a father and son is 7:3. If the sum of their ages is 60 years, find the difference in their ages.

**Q5.** A father's age is 4 times his son's age. After 16 years, he will be twice as old as his son. Find their present ages.

**Q6.** How many leap years are there in a century (100 years)?

**Q7.** If January 1, 2007 was a Monday, what day of the week was January 1, 2008?

**Q8.** At what time between 2 and 3 o'clock will the hands of a clock make a right angle?

**Q9.** A is 2 years older than B, who is twice as old as C. If the total of the ages of A, B and C be 27, then how old is B?

**Q10.** The calendar for the year 2009 will be the same for the year?

## 6. Answers & Detailed Explanations

**A1.** **130°**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  

$$\text{Angle} = |30(3) - 5.5(40)| = |90 - 220| = 130^\circ$$

**A2.** **21 9/11 minutes past 4**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  To be together, angle = 0.
  

$$30(4) - 5.5M = 0 \implies 120 = 5.5M \implies M = 240/11 = 21\frac{9}{11}\text{ minutes}$$

**A3.** **Saturday**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  61 days = 8 weeks + 5 odd days.
  Monday + 5 days = Saturday.

**A4.** **24 years**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Father = $7x$, Son = $3x$. $10x = 60 \implies x = 6$.
  Difference = $4x = 24$ years.

**A5.** **Father: 32 years, Son: 8 years**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Father = $4x$, Son = $x$.
  After 16 years: $4x + 16 = 2(x + 16) \implies 4x + 16 = 2x + 32 \implies 2x = 16 \implies x = 8$.
  Father = 32, Son = 8.

**A6.** **24**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  A normal century (e.g. 1901 to 2000) has 24 leap years, because the year 1900 is not a leap year. If the century year is divisible by 400 (e.g. 2000), it's a leap year. So usually a century has **24** leap years.

**A7.** **Tuesday**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  2007 is an ordinary year $\implies$ 1 odd day.
  January 1, 2008 will be Monday + 1 day = Tuesday.

**A8.** **27 3/11 minutes past 2**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  For right angle, difference in angle = 90°.
  

$$|30(2) - 5.5M| = 90 \implies 5.5M - 60 = 90 \implies 5.5M = 150 \implies M = 300/11 = 27\frac{3}{11}\text{ minutes}$$

**A9.** **10 years**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  C = $x$. B = $2x$. A = $2x + 2$.
  

$$x + 2x + 2x + 2 = 27 \implies 5x = 25 \implies x = 5$$

  B's age = $2x = 10$ years.

**A10.** **2015**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Count odd days from 2009 onwards:
  2009 (1), 2010 (1), 2011 (1), 2012 (2), 2013 (1), 2014 (1) $\implies$ Sum = 7 odd days.
  The calendar repeats when sum of odd days is a multiple of 7.
  So 2015 has the same calendar as 2009.
