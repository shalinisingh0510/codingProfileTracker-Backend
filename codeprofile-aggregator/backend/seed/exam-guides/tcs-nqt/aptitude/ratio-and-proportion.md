# Ratio and Proportion (TCS NQT Prep)

## 1. Core Principles

A **ratio** compares two quantities of the same kind by division. The ratio of $a$ to $b$ is written as $a:b = a/b$.
A **proportion** is an equation stating that two ratios are equal. If $a:b = c:d$, then $a/b = c/d$.

* $a$ and $d$ are called **extremes**.

* $b$ and $c$ are called **means**.

* **Cross-Product Rule**: Product of extremes = Product of means ($a \times d = b \times c$).

### Important Terms

* **Third Proportional**: If $a:b = b:c$, then $c$ is the third proportional to $a$ and $b$ ($c = b^2/a$).

* **Mean Proportional**: Between $a$ and $b$ is $\sqrt{ab}$.

* **Componendo and Dividendo Rule**:

  If $a/b = c/d$, then:
  

$$\frac{a+b}{a-b} = \frac{c+d}{c-d}$$

---

## 2. Key Scaling Shortcuts

When combining ratios, make the common term equal.

### Example: Combine $A:B$ and $B:C$

If $A:B = 2:3$ and $B:C = 4:5$.
The common term is $B$ (values are 3 and 4). Take LCM(3, 4) = 12.

* Multiply $A:B$ by 4 $\implies A:B = 8:12$

* Multiply $B:C$ by 3 $\implies B:C = 12:15$

* Combined Ratio $A:B:C = 8:12:15$.

## 3. Solved Examples

### Example 1:

Divide $720 among A, B, and C in the ratio 2:3:4.

* **Solution**:

  Sum of ratio parts = 2 + 3 + 4 = 9 parts.
  1 part = $720 / 9 = $80.
  A's share = 2 * 80 = $160.
  B's share = 3 * 80 = $240.
  C's share = 4 * 80 = $320.

### Example 2:

The ratio of ages of A and B is 4:5. Six years later, the ratio becomes 5:6. Find their present ages.

* **Solution**:

  Let their present ages be $4x$ and $5x$.
  After 6 years:
  

$$\frac{4x + 6}{5x + 6} = \frac{5}{6} \implies 6(4x + 6) = 5(5x + 6) \implies 24x + 36 = 25x + 30 \implies x = 6$$

  Present ages: A = 24 years, B = 30 years.

## 4. Practice Questions (10)

**Q1.** If A:B = 5:7 and B:C = 6:11, find the ratio A:B:C.

**Q2.** Find the mean proportional between 9 and 16.

**Q3.** Two numbers are in the ratio 3:5. If 9 is subtracted from each, the ratio becomes 12:23. Find the smaller number.

**Q4.** If 15% of A = 20% of B, find A:B.

**Q5.** In a bag, there are coins of 25p, 10p and 5p in the ratio 1:2:3. If the total money in the bag is $30, find the number of 5p coins.

**Q6.** If $a/b = 4/5$ and $b/c = 15/16$, find the value of $(c^2 - a^2) / (c^2 + a^2)$.

**Q7.** If A:B = 1:2, B:C = 3:4 and C:D = 5:6, find D:C:B:A.

**Q8.** A sum of $1,300 is divided among A, B, C and D such that A's share / B's share = B's share / C's share = C's share / D's share = 2/3. Find A's share.

**Q9.** The incomes of A and B are in the ratio 9:4 and their expenditures are in the ratio 7:3. If each saves $2,000, find their expenditures.

**Q10.** If $x:y = 3:4$, find the value of $(7x+3y) : (7x-3y)$.

## 5. Answers & Detailed Explanations

**A1.** **30:42:77**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Common term is B. Values are 7 and 6. LCM = 42.
  Multiply first ratio by 6: A:B = 30:42.
  Multiply second ratio by 7: B:C = 42:77.
  Combined ratio: **30:42:77**.

**A2.** **12**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Mean Proportional = $\sqrt{a \times b} = \sqrt{9 \times 16} = \sqrt{144} = 12$.

**A3.** **33**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Let the numbers be $3x$ and $5x$.
  

$$\frac{3x - 9}{5x - 9} = \frac{12}{23} \implies 23(3x - 9) = 12(5x - 9) \implies 69x - 207 = 60x - 108 \implies 9x = 99 \implies x = 11$$

  Smaller number = $3x = 33$.

**A4.** **4:3**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  $0.15A = 0.20B \implies A/B = 0.20 / 0.15 = 4/3 \implies A:B = 4:3$.

**A5.** **150 coins**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Let coins count be $1x, 2x, 3x$.
  Value of coins in dollars (or monetary units):
  

$$0.25(x) + 0.10(2x) + 0.05(3x) = 30 \implies 0.25x + 0.20x + 0.15x = 30 \implies 0.60x = 30 \implies x = 50$$

  Number of 5p coins = $3x = 3 \times 50 = 150$.

**A6.** **7/25**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  $a/b = 4/5$ and $b/c = 15/16 \implies a/c = (a/b) * (b/c) = (4/5) * (15/16) = 3/4$.
  Let a = 3, c = 4.
  

$$\text{Value} = \frac{c^2 - a^2}{c^2 + a^2} = \frac{16 - 9}{16 + 9} = \frac{7}{25}$$

**A7.** **48:40:30:15**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  A:B = 1:2
  B:C = 3:4 $\implies$ Combine: A:B:C = 3:6:8 (since LCM of 2 and 3 is 6).
  C:D = 5:6 $\implies$ Combine with C = 8 and C = 5 (LCM = 40).
  Multiply A:B:C by 5 $\implies$ 15:30:40.
  Multiply C:D by 8 $\implies$ 40:48.
  Combined A:B:C:D = 15:30:40:48.
  Reversing order for D:C:B:A gives **48:40:30:15**.

**A8.** **$160**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Let A = $8x$, B = $12x$, C = $18x$, D = $27x$ (derived from ratio 2:3 compounded).
  Sum = $8x + 12x + 18x + 27x = 65x$.
  $65x = 1300 \implies x = 20$.
  A's share = $8x = 160$.

**A9.** **Expenditures are $14,000 and $6,000**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Let incomes be $9x, 4x$ and expenditures be $7y, 3y$.
  $9x - 7y = 2000$ and $4x - 3y = 2000$.
  Solve for x and y:
  Multiply first by 3 and second by 7:
  $27x - 21y = 6000$
  $28x - 21y = 14000$
  Subtracting gives $x = 8000$.
  From $4x - 3y = 2000 \implies 32000 - 3y = 2000 \implies 3y = 30000 \implies y = 2000$.
  Expenditures = $7y = 14,000 and $3y = 6,000.

**A10.** **11:3**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Let x = 3, y = 4.
  

$$\frac{7x+3y}{7x-3y} = \frac{7(3)+3(4)}{7(3)-3(4)} = \frac{21+12}{21-12} = \frac{33}{9} = \frac{11}{3}$$
