# Percentages (TCS NQT Prep)

## 1. Core Concepts & Definitions

Percentage means **"per one hundred"** and is represented by the symbol **%**.

* To convert a percentage to a fraction, divide by 100:

  

$$\text{x}\% = \frac{\text{x}}{100}$$

* To convert a fraction to a percentage, multiply by 100:

  

$$\frac{\text{a}}{\text{b}} = \left(\frac{\text{a}}{\text{b}} \times 100\right)\%$$

### Percentage Change Formula

* **Percentage Increase** = 

$$\frac{\text{Increase}}{\text{Original Value}} \times 100$$

* **Percentage Decrease** = 

$$\frac{\text{Decrease}}{\text{Original Value}} \times 100$$

### Product Constancy Rule

If the price of a commodity increases by $R\%$, then the reduction in consumption so as not to increase the expenditure is:

$$\left(\frac{R}{100 + R} \times 100\right)\%$$

If the price decreases by $R\%$, the increase in consumption is:

$$\left(\frac{R}{100 - R} \times 100\right)\%$$

## 3. Advanced Successive Changes (Net Change)

When a value changes by $a\%$ and then successively by $b\%$, the net percentage change is given by the formula:

$$\text{Net Percentage Change} = a + b + \frac{a \times b}{100}\%$$

*Use positive values for increases/inflation and negative values for decreases/depreciation/discounts.*

### Same Percentage Change (+x% then -x%)

If a value is first increased by $x\%$ and then decreased by $x\%$, there is always a net **decrease** given by:

$$\text{Net Decrease} = \frac{x^2}{100}\%$$

## 4. Solved Examples

### Example 1:

If A's salary is 25% more than B's salary, by how much percentage is B's salary less than A's?

* **Solution**:

  Let B's salary be $100.
  A's salary = $100 + 25% of 100 = $125.
  B's salary is less than A's by = $125 - 100 = $25.
  Percentage less = $(25 / 125) * 100 = 20%.
  *Alternative Shortcut*: Using Product Constancy with $R = 25$:
  

$$\text{Percentage less} = \frac{25}{125} \times 100 = 20\%$$

### Example 2:

The population of a town increases by 10% in the first year and decreases by 10% in the second year. If the current population is 19,800, what was it 2 years ago?

* **Solution**:

  Let the population 2 years ago be $P$.
  After 1st year = $P * 1.10$.
  After 2nd year = $P * 1.10 * 0.90 = P * 0.99$.
  Given, $0.99 * P = 19,800 \implies P = 19,800 / 0.99 = 20,000$.

## 5. Practice Questions (10)

**Q1.** A shopkeeper increases the price of an item by 20% and then offers a discount of 10%. What is his net profit percentage?

**Q2.** If the price of petrol is increased by 25%, by what percentage must a driver reduce petrol consumption so his expenditure remains unchanged?

**Q3.** An examinee needs 40% marks to pass. He gets 178 marks and fails by 22 marks. What are the maximum marks?

**Q4.** If A is 50% of B, and B is 25% of C, what percent of C is A?

**Q5.** Due to a 20% reduction in the price of sugar, a buyer can buy 5 kg more for $100. Find the original price per kg.

**Q6.** In an election between two candidates, the candidate who gets 62% of the votes is elected by a majority of 144 votes. Find the total number of votes polled.

**Q7.** A student multiplied a number by 3/5 instead of 5/3. What is the percentage error in the calculation?

**Q8.** A man spends 75% of his income. His income increases by 20% and his expenditure increases by 10%. Find the percentage increase in his savings.

**Q9.** In a mixture of 40 liters, milk and water are in the ratio 3:1. How much water (in liters) must be added so the water becomes 60% of the new mixture?

**Q10.** The price of a car depreciates by 10% every year. If its present value is $9,000, what was its value 2 years ago?

## 6. Answers & Detailed Explanations

**A1.** **10% Profit**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Using successive percentage formula with $a = +20$ (increase) and $b = -10$ (discount/decrease):
  

$$\text{Net Change} = 20 - 10 + \frac{20 \times (-10)}{100} = 10 - 2 = 8\%$$

  *Wait! Let's correct the calculation*:
  

$$\text{Net Change} = 20 - 10 - \frac{200}{100} = 8\%$$

  So, net profit is **8%**.

**A2.** **20% Reduction**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Using the shortcut formula:
  

$$\text{Reduction}\% = \left(\frac{R}{100 + R}\right) \times 100 = \left(\frac{25}{125}\right) \times 100 = 20\%$$

**A3.** **500 marks**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Passing Marks required = 178 + 22 = 200 marks.
  Since 40% of Maximum Marks = 200:
  

$$\text{Maximum Marks} = \frac{200 \times 100}{40} = 500$$

**A4.** **12.5%**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Let C = 100.
  B = 25% of C = 25.
  A = 50% of B = 12.5.
  So, A is $(12.5 / 100) * 100 = 12.5%$ of C.

**A5.** **$5 per kg**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Let the original price be $x.
  New price = $0.8x$.
  Quantity bought originally = $100 / x$.
  Quantity bought now = $100 / 0.8x$.
  

$$\frac{100}{0.8x} - \frac{100}{x} = 5 \implies \frac{125}{x} - \frac{100}{x} = 5 \implies \frac{25}{x} = 5 \implies x = 5$$

  Original price was **$5 per kg**.

**A6.** **600 votes**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Winner gets 62% votes $\implies$ Loser gets 100% - 62% = 38% votes.
  Difference in percentage = 62% - 38% = 24%.
  Given, 24% of Total Votes = 144.
  

$$\text{Total Votes} = \frac{144 \times 100}{24} = 600$$

**A7.** **64% error**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Let the number be 15 (LCM of 3 and 5).
  Expected correct result = $15 * (5/3) = 25$.
  Obtained incorrect result = $15 * (3/5) = 9$.
  Error value = 25 - 9 = 16.
  

$$\text{Percentage Error} = \frac{\text{Error}}{\text{Correct Value}} \times 100 = \frac{16}{25} \times 100 = 64\%$$

**A8.** **50% increase**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Let original Income = 100, Expenditure = 75, Savings = 25.
  New Income = 120.
  New Expenditure = 75 + 10% of 75 = 82.5.
  New Savings = 120 - 82.5 = 37.5.
  Increase in Savings = 37.5 - 25 = 12.5.
  

$$\text{Percentage Increase} = \frac{12.5}{25} \times 100 = 50\%$$

**A9.** **35 liters**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  In 40 liters: Milk = 30L, Water = 10L.
  Let $w$ liters of water be added.
  New total volume = 40 + $w$.
  Water in new mixture = 10 + $w$.
  

$$\frac{10 + w}{40 + w} = 0.60 \implies 10 + w = 24 + 0.6w \implies 0.4w = 14 \implies w = 35\text{ liters}$$

**A10.** **$11,111.11**

* **Step-by-step Explanation**:

  Let value 2 years ago be $V$.
  Present Value = $V * 0.90 * 0.90 = 0.81V$.
  Given, $0.81V = 9,000 \implies V = 9,000 / 0.81 \approx 11,111.11$.

## 7. Last-minute Revision Card

* **Fraction Conversion**: Multiply by 100.

* **Percentage Conversion**: Divide by 100.

* **Net Successive Change**: $a + b + \frac{ab}{100}\%$

* **Product Constancy (price up, consumption down)**: $\frac{R}{100+R} \times 100\%$
