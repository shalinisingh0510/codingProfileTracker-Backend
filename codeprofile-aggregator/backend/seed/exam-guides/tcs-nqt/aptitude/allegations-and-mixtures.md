# Allegations and Mixtures (TCS NQT Aptitude)

## 1. Core Idea
Mixture concentration problems are **weighted averages**.

```text
Mean concentration = (sum of (quantity * concentration)) / (total quantity)
```

---

## 2. Allegation Method (Fastest)
To mix A% and B% to get M% (where A < M < B):

```text
Ratio of quantities (A : B) = (B - M) : (M - A)
```

**Step-by-step example**
Mix 20% and 50% to get 35%:
- Ratio = (50-35):(35-20) = 15:15 = 1:1

---

## 3. Replacement Trick (Mixture in a container)
If a vessel has V liters, and you remove x liters and replace with water, then after n operations:

```text
Final quantity of original liquid = V * (1 - x/V)^n
```

## 4. Practice Questions (10)
**Q1.** In what ratio should 20% and 50% solutions be mixed to get 35%?  
**Q2.** How many liters of water must be added to 10 liters of 30% milk to make it 20% milk?  
**Q3.** A shopkeeper mixes tea worth Rs. 200/kg and Rs. 300/kg to get a mixture worth Rs. 240/kg. Find ratio.  
**Q4.** In a 40L vessel containing milk, 10L is replaced by water once. Find milk left.  
**Q5.** In a 60L vessel, 12L is removed and replaced by water twice. Find milk left.  
**Q6.** A 25% solution and a 40% solution are mixed in ratio 3:2. Find final concentration.  
**Q7.** A mixture has milk and water in ratio 7:3. If 10L water is added, ratio becomes 7:5. Find initial mixture quantity.  
**Q8.** In what ratio should sugar solutions of 10% and 30% be mixed to get 18%?  
**Q9.** How many liters of 40% solution should be mixed with 60% solution to make 30L of 50% solution?  
**Q10.** A mixture contains alcohol and water in ratio 5:2. If 7L mixture has 5L alcohol, how much water?

---

## 5. Answers
**A1.** (50-35):(35-20) = 15:15 = 1:1  
**A2.** Milk amount = 10*0.30 = 3L. For 20%, total = 3/0.20 = 15L => add 5L water  
**A3.** Ratio = (300-240):(240-200) = 60:40 = 3:2  
**A4.** Milk left = 40*(1-10/40) = 40*(3/4)=30L  
**A5.** Milk left = 60*(1-12/60)^2 = 60*(4/5)^2 = 60*(16/25)=38.4L  
**A6.** (25*3 + 40*2)/5 = (75+80)/5 = 31%  
**A7.** Let initial = 10k, milk=7k, water=3k. Add 10 => water=3k+10, ratio 7:5 => 7k/(3k+10)=7/5 => 35k=21k+70 => k=5 => initial=50L  
**A8.** (30-18):(18-10)=12:8=3:2  
**A9.** Let x liters 40% and (30-x) liters 60% => 0.4x+0.6(30-x)=0.5*30 => x=15  
**A10.** If 7L has 5L alcohol => water=2L
